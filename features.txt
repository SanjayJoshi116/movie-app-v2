CINE DB — Feature List (v0.28.1)

DISCOVERY
- Browse Discover/Now Playing/Top Rated/Upcoming/Popular for movies, TV, anime
- Live search across movies, TV, and people (results use the same cards as the Movies/TV pages, watchlist button included); recent-searches dropdown, and the search box always shows the search in effect; the search term and tab live in the URL, so reload, a shared link or signing in brings back the same results
- People page has its own search box (in addition to the global search bar), kept in its address (/people?q=…) so Back and reload keep it; it never mixes with the global search, and the Movies/TV/Anime pages never come back in a search mode; a search with no matches says so
- Advanced filters: year range, TMDB rating, language, runtime, sort order; filters belong to one media type and reset when you switch between Movies, TV and the Anime tabs; on phones they open from a Filters button in the bottom nav; drawer edits (genres and "Include Adult Content" included) are a draft that applies together on Apply, in one request, and closing without Apply discards them; every drawer field is labelled for screen readers; a year range entered backwards is applied the right way round; category buttons are disabled while a filter decides the results; sorting by rating, release date or title skips barely-voted titles (no 10.0 from one vote), and Newest never shows titles dated after today
- Genre tag filtering: movie genres on movie pages, TV genres (e.g. "Sci-Fi & Fantasy") on TV/anime-TV; picking a genre always applies, even from a category tab; switching Anime between TV and Movies drops the genre pick and picks that mode's default category in the same, single request
- Browser Back / swipe-back from a detail or person page restores the browse page's category, loaded pages and scroll (Movies, TV, Anime and People), same as the in-app Back button
- Infinite scroll (Home, TV, Anime), up to TMDB's 500-page limit; a failed next page shows "Couldn't load more" with Retry and stops auto-loading until you retry; a failed first page never keeps loading behind the error screen
- Recently watched strip on Movies/TV pages
- Hero banner: trending title of the week

DETAIL PAGES
- Movie/TV detail: cast, videos, images, reviews, recommendations, similar titles, streaming availability, all loaded in one request; runtime/season/episode facts show only when known (no stray 0)
- Where to Watch lists Streaming, Rent and Buy for your region (defaults to your device's, change it in Edit Profile; kept on that device); when TMDB lists nothing there it shows US availability and says so, and with nothing anywhere it says the title isn't available in your region instead of showing empty headings; the movie certification and the Mark as Watched platform list follow the same region
- Trailers show a thumbnail with a play button; the YouTube player (privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie) loads only when you press play
- Movie and TV detail pages share the same section order/layout; up to 20 recommendation/similar cards; cast wraps to a centered grid (no horizontal scroll)
- Recommendations and Similar sections are both filtered to titles sharing at least half the opened title's genres (falls back to the unfiltered list rather than showing nothing)
- Backdrop images in the Images section open a full-size lightbox on click
- Info tooltips on non-obvious fields (TMDB Rating, Certification, Budget/Revenue currency)
- Add to / remove from custom lists via a shared modal (search existing lists, inline create-new-list, no dead end at zero lists); each list is a real, named checkbox (Tab + Space work)
- Mark as watched goes through a confirm modal with an editable runtime (prefilled: a movie's runtime, or for TV the whole series as episodes × episode runtime) and a streaming-platform pick (or "Other") — feeds Hours Watched / Platform Breakdown on Stats; the modal stays open and can't be closed while saving, and a failed save keeps what you entered
- Episode guide + episode progress tracker (TV); guide collapsed by default, episode list behind its own toggle; one-click "Next Episode" advance (handles season rollover, hides at the series finale); a failed progress load shows Retry instead of "not tracked", so saving can't overwrite real progress, and a double click sends one write
- Person pages: bio (Known For, Birthday/Deathday, IMDb, Website), full uncapped filmography (Movies/TV/Crew/Photos tabs, newest first; Crew shows one card per title with the jobs joined; the first tab follows what the person is known for; credits that fail to load show an error with Retry, never "No credits"), follow button; a website is a clickable link only for a real http(s) address
- A malformed movie/TV/person address (e.g. /movie/abc) shows "not found" without loading anything
- Back on a detail or person page always stays in the app: opened in a new tab or from another site, it goes to Movies, TV Shows or People
- Following page: search + sort followed people, "Recommended From People You Follow" section (each person's new and upcoming work, newest first, including what they directed, wrote or produced; talk-show appearances left out); a failed load shows an error with Retry, never "not following anyone"; a new follow shows first; Follow buttons wait until your followed list has loaded; a person with no saved name shows as "Person <id>"
- In-app notifications: bell icon (Sidebar/BottomNav) with unread badge for new releases (last 30 days) from every person you follow, polled every 3 minutes (each poll stays fast however many people you follow; a large cold follow list fills in over a few polls); keyboard-accessible dropdown that stays fully on screen at every screen size, visible "couldn't check for updates" state if a poll fails; covers work behind the camera too (follow a director and their new film shows up), never talk-show or news appearances; refreshes right after a follow or unfollow, and leaves out titles you've already watched; anything you hadn't seen when you last opened the bell shows as new, including a title TMDB adds late with an earlier date

LIBRARY
- Watchlist & Watched lists, tied to account; both paginate (12–96 per page, kept in the address), and removing the last items on the last page moves back a page
- Watch dates follow your own day: "today" uses this device's time zone, and each watch keeps the time zone it was logged in, so the Watched page, Stats heatmap and monthly charts never shift a day; a watch logged in another time zone shows a short zone label (e.g. 07-10-2026 · IST)
- Watchlist filters by media type and watched status; Watched filters by media type — both persist filter/search/sort state and have a one-click reset
- Watchlist's watched status is shared live with the Watched list (mark watched from any page, the Watchlist card reflects it immediately); marking watched opens the same runtime/platform-capture modal used everywhere else
- Library actions apply in the order you made them: an unmark during a pending mark unmarks, Clear All waits for pending adds (and adds made during it wait for it), a reload that raced a write reruns, saving a rating twice sends one request; library pages count as loading from the first render after sign-in, so ?page=N is kept and the empty state never flashes
- Search/sort/CSV export on Watchlist and Watched; exported CSVs are spreadsheet-safe (formula-looking titles open as text), open with correct accents in Excel (UTF-8 BOM) and re-import exactly
- Full backup (ZIP) and restore: watchlist, watched (runtime/platform/time zone), ratings + reviews, all lists incl. empty ones, followed people (with follow dates) and episode progress; restore keeps original dates (including list creation and item added dates, so lists keep their order) and adds items in batches (at most 500 rows or about 2 MB each, so a restore full of long reviews never fails a batch), never duplicates or overwrites, reports added/skipped/failed per section (a list it couldn't create counts as failed, even an empty one); lists that share a name stay separate; old v1/v2 backups still import; a file too large to be a backup is refused before it's unpacked
- Imports look up posters and scores at a steady pace under the TMDB limit (waiting out a rate limit instead of dropping the poster), show progress ("Looking up posters: 40 / 300") and report how many rows ended up without a poster
- Re-importing the Watched page's own CSV keeps each watch's date, time zone, runtime, platform and poster, and makes no TMDB lookups for rows that already have a poster and a score; an oversized poster value is dropped for that row only; a file that can't be read says so; watch times must be ISO 8601 with an offset (a bad one drops only that row), and a date alone is kept on that day
- Imports and backup restores stop as soon as you sign out (nothing lands in the next account) and their dialogs can't be closed mid-run, so an import can't run twice
- Ratings (0.5-10) + written reviews (up to 5000 characters, with a live count); remove a rating from the rating dialog; a failed save keeps your stars and review; the ten stars fit one line even on 320 px phones; the dialog can't be closed while a save is in flight; editing only the review doesn't re-send the score to TMDB
- Remove/unmark icons on library cards ask for confirmation, and their tooltip hides while it's open, so the confirm button is always tappable
- User Lists: create, rename, add/remove items, CSV export, CSV import (a CSV without dates keeps its file order); a new list shows first; create/edit can't double-submit; list cards open from the keyboard; each list has its own page (search/sort/type filter for its items; a list that doesn't exist shows "List not found") with a poster "theme image" on its grid card; deleting a list from its card asks first, and neither Delete nor Cancel opens the list; cards stay on screen while lists reload
- Stats dashboard: totals, movie/TV split, rating distribution, monthly activity, genre breakdown, rating-by-genre, platform breakdown, hours watched, reviews written, lists/watchlist-backlog counts, activity heatmap (keyboard-readable: one Tab stop, arrow keys move by day/week, the focused day is announced); half-star ratings fall into even buckets and averages round half up; monthly activity shows every month since your first watch, including empty ones; the TMDB average ignores unscored titles; genre charts fill in for any library size and count movie and TV genres as one family (Action and Adventure with TV's "Action & Adventure"); readable in both light and dark theme
- Recommendations (its tooltip says what they're built from: your own watches, ratings and follows, nothing from other users): personal history + "because you watched X" + followed-people picks; search + media-type filter; mark watched / add-to-watchlist directly from a recommendation card; a failed load retries in place, one failed group shows a warning with Retry without hiding the others, and a failed watched history shows an error instead of "no watched titles"; the "updating" state is accurate across server processes, and a refresh during a TMDB outage keeps your previous recommendations, and a title or person deleted from TMDB never freezes them; during a TMDB outage they're retried every 10 minutes at most, not on every poll; genres are matched per media type (a movie genre is never sent to TV), adding, changing or removing a rating refreshes them, titles rated 4 or below never seed "Because you watched" or shape your taste sections, titles you've rated are never recommended back to you, and a taste mixing a movie-only and a TV-only genre still gets titles, and a group that takes too long offers Retry instead of being saved as finished
- Release Calendar: the most popular releases of the next 7 days (every day of the week, not just the first), grouped by date, title search, sticky date headers, "Jump to Today", standards-compliant iCal export of exactly what's shown (the title filter applies); a failed load shows an error with Retry

AUTH
- JWT login/register; stay signed in for the full 7-day session (silent token refresh, works with several tabs open)
- Sign Out revokes the session on the server too, and clears everything account-related from the tab (cached recommendations, search results, filters, recent searches) so the next person to sign in sees none of it; browse settings (search term, include adult, genres, filters, sort) reset too; theme and sidebar preferences are kept
- Tabs stay in step: signing out in one tab signs out the others, and signing in as someone else in another tab reloads this one (no request ever goes out under the wrong account)
- A busy or unreachable server never signs you out; only a rejected session does
- Changing your password signs out every other device at once, including their already-issued access and any refresh that was in flight (this one stays signed in); completing a password reset signs out all devices
- Accounts without an email can edit their profile and change their password; clearing an email needs the current password
- The Edit Profile dialog always opens with your saved profile and empty password fields
- "Export All" never saves a partial backup: while something is still loading or failed to load, it says so instead of producing a file
- When a session expires for good, signing in again returns you to the page you were on
- After signing out, a late response for the previous account is never shown to whoever signs in next in that tab
- Changing your account email asks for your current password, rejects an email already used by another account (even when two sign-ups or changes race), and notifies your old address
- Changing your username asks for your current password too (it's how you sign in), and your email address is told about the change
- Email-based password reset; reset links expire after 1 hour
- Password policy: minimum length, common-password blocklist, no all-numeric passwords, and not too similar to your username, email or name (at sign-up, on a password change and on a reset; reinstated in 0.27.0 after being dropped in 0.15.3)
- Profile photo upload/remove (JPEG/PNG/WebP, 5MB max, checked by the image's actual content rather than its file name, and re-saved so location and camera details embedded in a photo are removed), shown wherever the avatar appears; a new photo shows at once, old or removed photos stop being served (also on account deletion), and a failed replace keeps the current photo; images over 16 megapixels are refused and stored photos are scaled to at most 1024 px a side
- Connecting TMDB completes only for the account that started it (a connect link sent by someone else is refused); reconnecting, disconnecting or deleting your account revokes the old TMDB session on TMDB's side, in the background after the change is saved, so a slow TMDB never blocks or half-applies it
- Edit Profile modal is tabbed: Account (incl. password strength meter), Data & TMDB (says "Checking TMDB connection…" until it knows), Danger Zone
- Failed login keeps username filled in (password cleared + refocused), and distinguishes bad-credentials from can't-reach-server errors; register, forgot-password and reset-password errors are readable too (an unreachable server is never reported as an expired reset link)
- Repeated wrong passwords for one account (its exact username, so `Alice` and `alice` are separate), from any number of devices or at once, pause sign-in for that account for a full hour (Retry-After shows the time left); wrong passwords on a profile update or account deletion count toward the same limit; a password reset clears it, and reset confirmation has its own rate limit
- Signed-in users who open the login, register or forgot-password page go straight on to where they were headed (no login-form flash); password-reset links still work while signed in

UI/UX
- Skeleton loaders (staggered fade-in), toast notifications (success only after the server confirms; double-clicks send one request, one toast)
- Every page shows loading, an error with in-place Retry, empty, or content — a failed load never looks like an empty library; detail pages degrade per section and show "not found" for bad ids
- Dark/light theme, both custom-designed (dark: cinema-dark navy; light: warm paper/cream) with a shared gold accent; icons (detail pages included), empty states, poster placeholders and the detail backdrop's fade follow the theme — Poppins font throughout, unified size scale: caption/body/emphasis/display
- Dates render as dd-mm-yyyy everywhere, independent of browser locale; one implausibly old imported date can never break Stats (imports reject dates before 1900)
- Fast pages: same-origin API in dev (no CORS preflights), identical TMDB requests shared, TMDB answers cached on the server (10 min for lists, 6 h for details, 24 h for genres), library loads 500 rows per request, images sized for display (w342 grid posters, w1280 backdrops)
- Keyboard accessible: every poster card that opens a page (including the Movies/TV/Anime grids, the hero's View Details and notification items) is a real link (Tab, Enter, middle/Ctrl-click opens a new tab) with a visible focus outline in both themes; icon-only buttons have screen-reader labels, including the phone drawer's "Edit profile" and "Sign out", and card buttons name their title ("Add to watchlist: Fight Club"); sidebar and bottom-nav entries are real links; each page has its own tab title ("Watchlist · CINE DB", a movie page uses its name), announced to screen readers after navigation; password fields tell password managers which is current and which is new; secondary and muted text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both themes; no duplicate element ids
- Unknown URLs show a "Page not found" page with a link back to Movies; a hand-edited page number, page size or tab in the address falls back to the default
- Framer Motion page transitions; with the OS "reduce motion" setting on, transitions, hover effects, the Stats count-up, the Stats charts and the title marquee don't animate, and page changes and Jump to Today scroll instantly; long card titles end in an ellipsis with the full title as a tooltip
- Installs as "CINE DB" with its own gold "C" app icon and favicon (web app manifest + page description); the browser tab reads "CINE DB" too
- Responsive across screen sizes: full sidebar (desktop, 992px+), icon-only collapsed rail (tablet, 768-991px), bottom nav (phone, under 768px); no fixed-width overflow on narrow phones (auth cards, filter-bar inputs)
- Desktop sidebar has a manual collapse toggle (folds to the same icon rail tablet uses); preference remembered; in the rail, the footer controls sit in a compact 2-column icon grid
- Sidebar stays pinned in place while scrolling; nav icons briefly flash their tooltip label on click, most useful on the icon-only tablet rail and phone bottom nav; the section you're in stays highlighted on its sub-pages (a list's page highlights My Lists)

SECURITY & OPERATIONS
- Production Docker stack runs the backend as an unprivileged user from the image only (no host-mounted code or migrations)
- Content-Security-Policy, HSTS (behind HTTPS) and other security headers on every response
- The TMDB API key, TMDB session ids (guest sessions included) and request tokens are redacted from every server log line, and unexpected server errors are logged with a traceback in production
- Password-reset links and TMDB callback tokens never appear in the web server's access log; production refuses to start without a configured email backend (so reset links are never printed to the log) or without the app's real address (so reset links never point at localhost)
- Imports of unknown titles can't flood TMDB: missing title details are filled at most 100 at a time (recommendations at most 200 per refresh), every account's background title lookups are capped at 1,000 a day, and titles TMDB doesn't know are remembered permanently
- A redeploy never leaves an open tab on a blank page (the entry page is revalidated; a missing old file is a clean 404)
- The app's scripts and styles are served compressed, and their hashed files are cached by the browser for a year
- The database container gets only the database password (kept in its own .env.db), none of the app's other secrets
- Followed people's TMDB credits are cached apart from rate-limit counters, so following many people can never reset anyone's limits, and a notifications check stays light however many people you follow
- Endpoints that wait on TMDB (proxy, notifications, followed-people recommendations, Stats genre lookup, TMDB connect, rating sync) each answer within a fixed time budget under the server's request timeout; every TMDB call has a wall-clock limit covering all connection attempts
- Followed-people recommendations are rate limited per user, like the other endpoints that spend TMDB quota; ratings outside 0–10 are refused, so they can never break Stats; a stalled mail server can't half-apply an email change
- Production reuses database connections (60 s) instead of connecting per request
- The backend serves requests on threads (3 processes × 8 threads), so a few requests waiting on a slow TMDB never block sign-in or the rest of the app; background title-detail lookups run at most 2 at a time per process; expired sign-in records are cleaned up at every start, without ever holding startup up
- No Django admin site: the only login is the app's own, with its rate limits
- TMDB browsing limits apply per signed-in user, so people sharing one network don't share a limit; a malformed time zone never causes an error
- A streaming-provider link from TMDB is used only if it's a real http(s) address
- Node 22 and the Python linter are pinned, so local, CI and Docker builds use the same versions; a fresh clone runs `npm run dev` with any Python environment (set DJANGO_PYTHON), and the example env files list every setting; CI never retries a failed test, lints the e2e specs, and builds and checks both Docker images
