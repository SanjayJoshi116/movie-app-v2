CINE DB — Feature List (v0.15.4)

DISCOVERY
- Browse Discover/Now Playing/Top Rated/Upcoming/Popular for movies, TV, anime
- Live search across movies, TV, and people; recent-searches dropdown
- People page has its own search box (in addition to the global search bar)
- Advanced filters: year range, TMDB rating, language, runtime, sort order
- Genre tag filtering
- Infinite scroll (Home, TV, Anime)
- Recently watched strip on Movies/TV pages
- Hero banner: trending title of the week

DETAIL PAGES
- Movie/TV detail: cast, videos, images, reviews, recommendations, similar titles, streaming availability
- Movie and TV detail pages share the same section order/layout; up to 20 recommendation/similar cards; cast wraps to a centered grid (no horizontal scroll)
- Recommendations and Similar sections are both filtered to titles sharing at least half the opened title's genres (falls back to the unfiltered list rather than showing nothing)
- Backdrop images in the Images section open a full-size lightbox on click
- Info tooltips on non-obvious fields (TMDB Rating, Certification, Budget/Revenue currency)
- Add to / remove from custom lists via a shared modal (search existing lists, inline create-new-list, no dead end at zero lists)
- Mark as watched goes through a confirm modal that auto-fetches runtime and lets you pick the streaming platform (or "Other") — feeds Hours Watched / Platform Breakdown on Stats
- Episode guide + episode progress tracker (TV); guide collapsed by default, episode list behind its own toggle
- Person pages: bio (Known For, Birthday/Deathday, IMDb, Website), full uncapped filmography (Movies/TV/Photos tabs), follow button
- Following page: search + sort followed people, "Recommended From People You Follow" section

LIBRARY
- Watchlist & Watched lists, tied to account
- Watchlist filters by media type and watched status; Watched filters by media type — both persist filter/search/sort state and have a one-click reset
- Watchlist's watched status is shared live with the Watched list (mark watched from any page, the Watchlist card reflects it immediately); marking watched opens the same runtime/platform-capture modal used everywhere else
- Search/sort/CSV export on Watchlist and Watched
- Ratings (0.5-10) + written reviews
- User Lists: create, rename, add/remove items, CSV export, CSV import; each list has its own page (search/sort/type filter for its items) with a poster "theme image" on its grid card
- Stats dashboard: totals, movie/TV split, rating distribution, monthly activity, genre breakdown, rating-by-genre, platform breakdown, hours watched, reviews written, lists/watchlist-backlog counts, activity heatmap
- Recommendations: personal history + "because you watched X" + followed-people picks; search + media-type filter; mark watched / add-to-watchlist directly from a recommendation card
- Release Calendar: 7-day lookahead, grouped by date, title search, sticky date headers, "Jump to Today", iCal export

AUTH
- JWT login/register
- Email-based password reset
- Password policy: minimum length, common-password blocklist, no all-numeric passwords (no longer blocks passwords similar to your username/email, by design — see CHANGELOG 0.15.3)
- Profile photo upload/remove (JPEG/PNG/WebP, 5MB max), shown wherever the avatar appears
- Edit Profile modal is tabbed: Account (incl. password strength meter), Data & TMDB, Danger Zone

UI/UX
- Skeleton loaders (staggered fade-in), toast notifications
- Dark/light theme, both custom-designed (dark: cinema-dark navy; light: warm paper/cream) with a shared gold accent — Poppins font throughout, unified size scale: caption/body/emphasis/display
- Dates render as dd-mm-yyyy everywhere, independent of browser locale
- Framer Motion page transitions
- Responsive across screen sizes: full sidebar (desktop, 992px+), icon-only collapsed rail (tablet, 768-991px), bottom nav (phone, under 768px); no fixed-width overflow on narrow phones (auth cards, filter-bar inputs)
- Desktop sidebar has a manual collapse toggle (folds to the same icon rail tablet uses); preference remembered
- Sidebar stays pinned in place while scrolling; nav icons briefly flash their tooltip label on click, most useful on the icon-only tablet rail and phone bottom nav
