CINE DB — Feature List (v0.19.0)

DISCOVERY
- Browse Discover/Now Playing/Top Rated/Upcoming/Popular for movies, TV, anime
- Live search across movies, TV, and people; recent-searches dropdown
- People page has its own search box (in addition to the global search bar)
- Advanced filters: year range, TMDB rating, language, runtime, sort order; filters belong to one media type and reset when you switch between Movies, TV and the Anime tabs; on phones they open from a Filters button in the bottom nav
- Genre tag filtering: movie genres on movie pages, TV genres (e.g. "Sci-Fi & Fantasy") on TV/anime-TV; picking a genre always applies, even from a category tab
- Browser Back / swipe-back from a detail page restores the browse page's category, loaded pages and scroll, same as the in-app Back button
- Infinite scroll (Home, TV, Anime)
- Recently watched strip on Movies/TV pages
- Hero banner: trending title of the week

DETAIL PAGES
- Movie/TV detail: cast, videos, images, reviews, recommendations, similar titles, streaming availability
- Movie and TV detail pages share the same section order/layout; up to 20 recommendation/similar cards; cast wraps to a centered grid (no horizontal scroll)
- Recommendations and Similar sections are both filtered to titles sharing at least half the opened title's genres (falls back to the unfiltered list rather than showing nothing)
- Backdrop images in the Images section open a full-size lightbox on click
- Info tooltips on non-obvious fields (TMDB Rating, Certification, Budget/Revenue currency)
- Add to / remove from custom lists via a shared modal (search existing lists, inline create-new-list, no dead end at zero lists)
- Mark as watched goes through a confirm modal that auto-fetches runtime and lets you pick the streaming platform (or "Other") — feeds Hours Watched / Platform Breakdown on Stats
- Episode guide + episode progress tracker (TV); guide collapsed by default, episode list behind its own toggle; one-click "Next Episode" advance (handles season rollover, hides at the series finale)
- Person pages: bio (Known For, Birthday/Deathday, IMDb, Website), full uncapped filmography (Movies/TV/Photos tabs), follow button
- Following page: search + sort followed people, "Recommended From People You Follow" section
- In-app notifications: bell icon (Sidebar/BottomNav) with unread badge for new releases (last 30 days) from every person you follow, polled every 3 minutes; keyboard-accessible dropdown that stays fully on screen at every screen size, visible "couldn't check for updates" state if a poll fails

LIBRARY
- Watchlist & Watched lists, tied to account
- Watch dates follow your own day: "today" uses this device's time zone, and each watch keeps the time zone it was logged in, so the Watched page, Stats heatmap and monthly charts never shift a day; a watch logged in another time zone shows a short zone label (e.g. 07-10-2026 · IST)
- Watchlist filters by media type and watched status; Watched filters by media type — both persist filter/search/sort state and have a one-click reset
- Watchlist's watched status is shared live with the Watched list (mark watched from any page, the Watchlist card reflects it immediately); marking watched opens the same runtime/platform-capture modal used everywhere else
- Search/sort/CSV export on Watchlist and Watched
- Full backup (ZIP) and restore: watchlist, watched (runtime/platform/time zone), ratings + reviews, all lists incl. empty ones; restore keeps original dates, never duplicates or overwrites, reports added/skipped/failed per section; old backups still import
- Ratings (0.5-10) + written reviews; remove a rating from the rating dialog; a failed save keeps your stars and review
- User Lists: create, rename, add/remove items, CSV export, CSV import; each list has its own page (search/sort/type filter for its items) with a poster "theme image" on its grid card
- Stats dashboard: totals, movie/TV split, rating distribution, monthly activity, genre breakdown, rating-by-genre, platform breakdown, hours watched, reviews written, lists/watchlist-backlog counts, activity heatmap; genre charts fill in for any library size; readable in both light and dark theme
- Recommendations: personal history + "because you watched X" + followed-people picks; search + media-type filter; mark watched / add-to-watchlist directly from a recommendation card; a failed load retries in place
- Release Calendar: 7-day lookahead, grouped by date, title search, sticky date headers, "Jump to Today", iCal export

AUTH
- JWT login/register; stay signed in for the full 7-day session (silent token refresh, works with several tabs open)
- Sign Out revokes the session on the server too, and clears everything account-related from the tab (cached recommendations, search results, filters, recent searches) so the next person to sign in sees none of it; theme and sidebar preferences are kept
- Changing your password signs out every other device at once, including their already-issued access and any refresh that was in flight (this one stays signed in); completing a password reset signs out all devices
- Changing your account email asks for your current password, rejects an email already used by another account, and notifies your old address
- Email-based password reset; reset links expire after 1 hour
- Password policy: minimum length, common-password blocklist, no all-numeric passwords (no longer blocks passwords similar to your username/email, by design — see CHANGELOG 0.15.3)
- Profile photo upload/remove (JPEG/PNG/WebP, 5MB max, checked by the image's actual content rather than its file name), shown wherever the avatar appears
- Edit Profile modal is tabbed: Account (incl. password strength meter), Data & TMDB, Danger Zone
- Failed login keeps username filled in (password cleared + refocused), and distinguishes bad-credentials from can't-reach-server errors
- Signed-in users who open the login, register or forgot-password page go straight on to where they were headed (no login-form flash); password-reset links still work while signed in

UI/UX
- Skeleton loaders (staggered fade-in), toast notifications (success only after the server confirms; double-clicks send one request, one toast)
- Every page shows loading, an error with in-place Retry, empty, or content — a failed load never looks like an empty library; detail pages degrade per section and show "not found" for bad ids
- Dark/light theme, both custom-designed (dark: cinema-dark navy; light: warm paper/cream) with a shared gold accent — Poppins font throughout, unified size scale: caption/body/emphasis/display
- Dates render as dd-mm-yyyy everywhere, independent of browser locale
- Keyboard accessible: every poster card that opens a page is a real link (Tab, Enter, middle/Ctrl-click opens a new tab) with a visible focus outline in both themes; icon-only buttons have screen-reader labels
- Unknown URLs show a "Page not found" page with a link back to Movies
- Framer Motion page transitions
- Installs as "CINE DB" with its own gold "C" app icon and favicon (web app manifest + page description)
- Responsive across screen sizes: full sidebar (desktop, 992px+), icon-only collapsed rail (tablet, 768-991px), bottom nav (phone, under 768px); no fixed-width overflow on narrow phones (auth cards, filter-bar inputs)
- Desktop sidebar has a manual collapse toggle (folds to the same icon rail tablet uses); preference remembered; in the rail, the footer controls sit in a compact 2-column icon grid
- Sidebar stays pinned in place while scrolling; nav icons briefly flash their tooltip label on click, most useful on the icon-only tablet rail and phone bottom nav

SECURITY & OPERATIONS
- Production Docker stack runs the backend as an unprivileged user from the image only (no host-mounted code or migrations)
- Content-Security-Policy, HSTS (behind HTTPS) and other security headers on every response
- The TMDB API key is redacted from every server log line, and unexpected server errors are logged with a traceback in production
